French Toast Recipe

Ingredients
4 slices of bread
2 eggs
1/2 cup milk
1 tablespoon sugar
1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
1 teaspoon vanilla extract
1 tablespoon butter

Instructions
Whisk the eggs, milk, sugar, cinnamon, and vanilla extract in a bowl.
Dip each slice of bread into the mixture, coating both sides.
Heat the butter in a pan over medium heat.
Place the soaked bread in the pan.
Cook for 2–3 minutes on each side, until golden brown.
Remove from the pan and repeat with the remaining slices.
Serve warm with maple syrup, fresh fruit, honey, or powdered sugar.
