#### **Ingredients**

* 2 cups (250 g) all-purpose flour  
* 2 tbsp sugar  
* 1 tbsp baking powder  
* ½ tsp salt  
* 2 eggs  
* 1¾ cups (420 ml) milk  
* ½ cup (115 g) melted butter  
* 1 tsp vanilla extract

#### **Method**

1. **Mix dry ingredients:** Combine flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.  
2. **Mix wet ingredients:** Whisk eggs, milk, melted butter, and vanilla.  
3. **Combine:** Pour wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and mix until just combined. Don't overmix—some small lumps are fine.  
4. **Heat waffle iron:** Preheat and lightly grease it.  
5. **Cook:** Pour in enough batter to cover the plates without overflowing. Cook for **3–5 minutes**, or until golden and crisp.  
6. **Serve:** Enjoy immediately with maple syrup, butter, fresh berries, bananas, or whipped cream.

**For extra-crispy waffles:** Replace ¼ cup of the flour with **¼ cup cornstarch**.
